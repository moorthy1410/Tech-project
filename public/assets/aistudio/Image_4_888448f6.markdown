## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a contemporary, high-precision enterprise software aesthetic. Built for multi-user collaboration across product, research, and executive teams, the interface projects clarity, operational velocity, and trust.

- **Design Philosophy:** Modern SaaS minimalism combined with low-contrast structural outlines and subtle tonal layering. Whitespace functions as an active structural element, preventing cognitive fatigue in high-density survey builders and feedback dashboards.
- **Tone & Atmosphere:** Confident, analytical, frictionless, and orderly.
- **Target Audience:** Product managers, UX researchers, CX leads, and enterprise administrators orchestrating continuous customer feedback loops and high-volume data collection.

## Colors

The palette employs a purposeful separation between core navigation, feedback completion health metrics, role taxonomy, and crisp neutral structural layers.

- **Primary (`#4F46E5`):** High-density indigo utilized for key focal actions, interactive navigation items, selected states, and core progress indicators.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#10B981`):** Precision emerald reserved for positive delta rates, live survey publication statuses, sentiment health metrics, and successful completion states.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6`):** Violet tone designated specifically for elevated organizational tiers and administrative management badges.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC`
  - Elevated card and panel surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle structural border: `#E2E8F0`
  - Secondary border & divider: `#F1F5F9`
  - Primary text & headlines: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - Secondary body & metadata: `#475569` (Slate 600)
  - Muted captions & icons: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
- **Role Taxonomies:**
  - Administrator: Background `#F5F3FF`, Text `#6D28D9`, Border `#DDD6FE`
  - Standard User / Contributor: Background `#EFF6FF`, Text `#1D4ED8`, Border `#BFDBFE`
  - Analyst / Viewer: Background `#F0FDF4`, Text `#047857`, Border `#BBF7D0`

## Typography

Typography prioritizes tabular clarity, crisp form labels, and effortless visual parsing across dense data reports.

- **Primary Typeface:** `Inter` is applied universally across display headers, body layouts, dynamic metrics, and administrative tags to ensure unified vertical rhythm and predictable horizontal tracking.
- **Numerical Treatment:** For metric scorecards, survey response totals, and NPS breakdowns, apply OpenType tabular numbers (`tnum`) to maintain structural balance during live data polling.
- **Letter Spacing:** Maintain standard tracking for body copy; apply `-0.015em` to headings above `20px` to enhance density and authoritative tone. Use `+0.02em` for uppercase tags and micro-labels.
